Proposed, not built. No judge yet; it is written by hand, by a person, when this problem is picked.

After M_6_1 and M_6_2: nodes repel and edges pull until a slice of a fixed offline Wikipedia link graph untangles.

**The agent (yours).** The force rules and the cooling schedule. The judge runs the steps and reads the final positions.

**The check.** Every node inside the frame, no two nodes closer than their radius, within the budget.

**The score.** Minus edge crossings, plus a small term for even edge lengths.
